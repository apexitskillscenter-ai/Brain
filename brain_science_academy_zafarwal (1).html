About Brain Science Academy

Empowering Students Through Advanced Academic Coaching

Brain Science Academy Zafarwal is dedicated to raising the standard of education in the region. Under the expert guidance of Principal Sir Shahid Javed, our institution fosters an environment of discipline, curiosity, and high academic performance.

We offer specialized classes ranging from the 8th grade up to intermediate levels (FSc, ICS, FA). With our separate, highly secure, and well-organized campuses for boys and girls, students receive focused attention and an ideal atmosphere for continuous growth.
